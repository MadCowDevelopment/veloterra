# 2.2 Quality Goals

<details>
<summary>Documentation Hints</summary>

List the highest-priority quality goals and make their architectural relevance explicit.
</details>

| Priority | Quality Goal | Architectural Relevance |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Offline ride usability | App shell and downloaded basemap assets remain available through Workbox caches; ride data remains local. |
| 2 | Data durability | IndexedDB and persisted Zustand stores retain progress across sessions; cloud sync is optional. |
| 3 | Privacy | Progress is device-local by default; signed-in cloud rows are isolated by user identity and RLS. |
| 4 | Ride interaction responsiveness | GPS processing, HUD updates, H3 reveal, and map following occur client-side without a server round trip. |
| 5 | Evolvability | UI, map, state, domain constants, persistence, and integration concerns are separated into focused modules. |

Concrete quality scenarios and current evidence are defined in [Section 12](../12-Quality-Requirements/12.1-Quality-Requirements-Overview.md).
